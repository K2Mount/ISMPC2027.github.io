About ISMPC2027

The 9th International Symposium on Monolayer-Protected Clusters will bring the international cluster science community to Singapore from 1 to 4 August 2027.

About the ISMPC Series

ISMPC is a long-standing international symposium series dedicated to advances in atomically precise metal nanoclusters and related systems. Since the inaugural meeting in Finland in 2008, the symposium has brought together researchers worldwide to discuss recent developments in cluster synthesis, structure, properties, and emerging applications.

ISMPC2027 in Singapore

ISMPC2027 will bring the international cluster science community to Singapore from 1 to 4 August 2027. Hosted at Shaw Foundation Alumni House on the NUS Kent Ridge campus, the symposium will provide a focused setting for scientific exchange, collaboration, and discussion of emerging directions in monolayer-protected clusters and atomically precise nanomaterials.

The local organization is led by Xie Group @ NUS in the Department of Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering. Prof. Jianping Xie serves as Conference Chair, supported by Dr. Zhucheng Yang as Conference Secretary.
