Attendee support

Planning an inclusive visit

Practical support information for delegates preparing to attend ISMPC2027 in Singapore.

Before you travel

Tell us what you need

Arrangements are still being finalized. Contact the organizing team early if an accessibility, dietary, or documentation need may affect your participation.

Accessibility

Let us know about mobility, hearing, visual, sensory, or other access requirements. We will review requests with the venue and respond with the available arrangements.

Dietary requirements

Dietary information will be collected through the registration process once it opens. For advance planning questions, contact the organizing team.

Visa-support requests

If you may need conference documentation for a visa application, contact the organizers after registration instructions are published. Any supporting document does not guarantee visa approval.

Check Singapore visa requirements

Community standard

Code of conduct

ISMPC2027 is intended to be a respectful, professional, and welcoming scientific meeting. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or disruptive conduct is not acceptable in conference sessions, social activities, digital channels, or other event spaces.

Concerns may be raised with the organizing team. Reports will be handled as discreetly as practicable and reviewed in the context of participant safety and fair process.

Images & personal information

Photography and privacy

Event photography or recording may take place in designated areas. Any participant instructions, restricted areas, or opt-out process will be communicated before the meeting.

Registration information should be collected only for conference administration and participant support. Detailed registration and privacy terms will be published with the registration process.

Need assistance?

Describe the support you are requesting and any timing constraints. Please avoid sending passport scans, medical records, or other sensitive documents unless the organizers specifically request them through an appropriate channel.
