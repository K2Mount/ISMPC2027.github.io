Singapore · 2027

A focused international meeting for cluster science

ISMPC2027 brings together researchers working across atomically precise clusters, nanochemistry, catalysis, spectroscopy, theory and emerging applications. Join the community at NUS for four days of invited talks, contributed presentations and scientific exchange.
