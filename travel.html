Conference venue

Main scientific venue

Shaw Foundation Alumni House

ISMPC2027 will be hosted at Shaw Foundation Alumni House (SFAH), a dedicated event venue on the National University of Singapore's Kent Ridge campus.

The scientific programme will be centred on two principal spaces within SFAH: the Auditorium and the Lavender Seminar Room. Detailed room assignments will be published with the final programme.

  • Main spacesAuditorium and Lavender Seminar Room

Where to stay

Our recommended option

The Ridge, NUS

The Ridge is the preferred accommodation for eligible ISMPC2027 delegates. This dedicated NUS guest property is on campus and will also host the conference welcome reception.

Delegates who wish to stay at The Ridge should select this option during registration. The organizing team will consolidate eligible requests as a group booking and send the applicable reservation link and instructions once arrangements are confirmed.

Getting to NUS

Taxi or private hire

The simplest option with luggage. Enter the full destination rather than only “NUS”:

  • Shaw Foundation Alumni House, 11 Kent Ridge Drive
  • The Ridge, 17 Computing Drive
  • Check live traffic and fare estimates before departure

MRT and campus transfer

Kent Ridge MRT (Circle Line) is the main rail gateway to the campus. Continue by NUS Internal Shuttle Bus, public bus, taxi, or private hire.

  • SFAH is a short walk from the AS5 campus shuttle stop
  • Routes can change; use the current NUS NextBus or uNivUS information
  • Allow extra time during peak periods
NUS transport

To The Ridge

From Kent Ridge MRT, NUS shuttles connect to stops within walking distance of The Ridge.

  • COM3 via service D2
  • Central Library via service A1
  • Opp NUSS via service A2
Official directions

Airport arrival

Changi Airport is connected to the city by MRT, taxi, and private-hire services. Public transport is economical but requires transfers for NUS.

  • Follow official airport signs for transport services
  • Use licensed taxi queues or recognized ride-hailing apps
  • Build in time for immigration and baggage collection

Entry and visa information

Check visa requirements

Entry requirements depend on nationality and travel document. Confirm the latest requirements with Singapore's Immigration & Checkpoints Authority.
