Venue

Shaw Foundation Alumni House

ISMPC2027 will take place at SFAH on the National University of Singapore's Kent Ridge campus.

Main conference venue

Shaw Foundation Alumni House (SFAH)

SFAH is a dedicated NUS venue for alumni, university, and community events. ISMPC2027 scientific sessions and associated conference activities will be based here, principally in the Auditorium and Lavender Seminar Room.

  • Address11 Kent Ridge Drive, Singapore 119244
  • CampusNUS Kent Ridge Campus
  • Main spacesAuditorium and Lavender Seminar Room

Conference flow

Scientific sessions

The main programme will be hosted in the SFAH Auditorium and Lavender Seminar Room. The final schedule will assign plenary, keynote, and contributed sessions to each space.

Breaks and networking

Onsite arrangements for registration, refreshments, poster activities, and informal discussion will be announced with the delegate guide.

Welcome reception

The conference welcome reception will be held at The Ridge. Timing and room details will be included in the final programme.

Reception & recommended stay

The Ridge, NUS

The Ridge is our preferred accommodation for eligible ISMPC2027 delegates and will also host the conference welcome reception.

Delegates who wish to stay at The Ridge should select this option during registration. Eligible requests will be coordinated as a group booking, after which the organizing team will issue the applicable reservation link and instructions. Rooms remain subject to NUS eligibility and availability.

Detailed logistics to follow

Room assignments, access instructions, registration-desk location, poster layout, catering details, and accessibility guidance will be published once the onsite plan is finalized.
